About the lab

Design and manufacturing technology spanning nano to macro scale.

The Intelligent Manufacturing Technology and PEMFC Laboratory researches design and manufacturing technologies that reach from the nanoscale to the macroscale. We synthesize and characterize metal complexes, MOF nanoparticles, metal oxides, and 1D/2D/3D materials, and apply them to electrocatalysis — hydrogen and oxygen evolution reactions — and electrochemical sensing.

On the hardware side, we manufacture proton-exchange membrane (PEM) fuel cell devices and the precision components behind them, working toward green hydrogen production and green automotive technology. The lab sits within the Future & Automotive Engineering Department at Kongju National University, bringing materials chemistry and precision manufacturing together under one roof.

Research focus

Six connected threads, spanning materials synthesis through to intelligent manufacturing systems.

Materials

Synthesis & characterization

Metal complexes, MOF nanoparticles, metal oxides, and 1D/2D/3D nanostructures designed for catalytic and sensing performance.

Electrochemistry

Electrocatalysis & sensing

Hydrogen and oxygen evolution (HER/OER), oxygen reduction (ORR), and electrochemical sensors built on engineered nanomaterial surfaces.

Energy storage

Supercapacitors & batteries

Electrode materials and architectures for next-generation energy storage devices.

Fuel cells

PEMFC hardware

Flow-field design and 3D-printed bipolar plates for proton-exchange membrane fuel cells, validated with CFD and physical testing.

Nanotechnology

Nanowire networks

Ag/Cu nanowire synthesis, patterning, and laser processing for flexible, transparent conductive films and electronic devices.

Manufacturing

Intelligent manufacturing

CAD/CAM, precision machining, and additive manufacturing paired with machine vision and AI-driven process monitoring.

Team

A lab of materials chemists, mechanical engineers, and simulation specialists working across two continents.
