Our expertise

At the KNU Intelligent Manufacturing Technology and Fuel Cell Laboratory, our core expertise is in CAD/CAM systems and precision manufacturing. We build AI models directly from field manufacturing data for process optimization, design optimization, and fault diagnosis, and extend that work into digital twins that mirror real production systems. We apply this same manufacturing-intelligence approach to a focused application area: synthesizing electrocatalysts and nanomaterials, and precision-manufacturing the membrane electrode assemblies, bipolar plates, and stack components that turn them into working PEM fuel cells — connecting nano-to-macro materials research with intelligent manufacturing, and contributing to green hydrogen production and green car technology.
