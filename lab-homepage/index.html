Intelligent Manufacturing Technology & PEMFC Laboratory

We work from nanowire networks to fuel-cell hardware — synthesizing electrocatalysts, building conductive nanomaterial films, and manufacturing the precision components that turn them into working hydrogen energy devices.

Our expertise

At the KNU Intelligent Manufacturing Technology and Fuel Cell Laboratory, we are at the forefront of pioneering design and manufacturing technologies. Our research spans from nano to macro scales, encompassing the synthesis and characterization of advanced materials such as metal complexes, MOF nanoparticles, metal oxides, and 1D/2D/3D materials. These materials are applied in various domains, including electrocatalytic applications (hydrogen and oxygen evolution reactions), electrochemical sensing, and PEM fuel cell device fabrication. Additionally, we focus on the production of green hydrogen, contributing to green car technology at the research center of Kongju National University, Cheonan-si Campus.
