## 2. Research summary

- **Description**: I am currently engaged in research focused on reducing cooling times by integrating Triply Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS) cooling channels into metal 3D-printed injection molds. Through the coupling of thermal-fluid and structural simulations, my work aims to derive the optimal design conditions that successfully resolve the trade-off between the cooling efficiency required for rapid thermal dissipation and the structural rigidity necessary to withstand injection pressures.
