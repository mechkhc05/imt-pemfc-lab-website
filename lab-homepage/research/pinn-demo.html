PEMFC PINN Operating-Condition Explorer

An interactive demo of the lab's physics-informed neural network (PINN) surrogate model. Move any slider and watch predicted performance update live — entirely in your browser.

The model was trained on CFD simulation data spanning realistic PEMFC operating envelopes, and predicts four performance quantities — current density, power density, system efficiency, and O2 distribution uniformity (ODU) — as a function of eight operating conditions. Adjust any condition on the left; each chart on the right re-sweeps the full 0.4–0.9 V cell-voltage range and marks where the current voltage slider sits on that curve. This tool extends the surrogate-modeling approach behind our lab's comparative PEMFC ML-surrogate study (Ngo et al., J. Ind. Eng. Chem., 2025), and is offered here for exploration — it is a trained surrogate, not a substitute for experimental or CFD validation.
