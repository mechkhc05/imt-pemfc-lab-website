Research

Six connected threads, spanning materials synthesis through to intelligent manufacturing systems.

Synthesis & Characterization

Metal complexes, MOF nanoparticles, metal oxides, and 1D/2D/3D nanostructures.

Electrocatalysis & Sensing

Hydrogen and oxygen evolution reactions, and electrochemical sensing.

PEM Fuel Cell Hardware

Manufacturing proton-exchange membrane fuel cell devices and flow-field components.

Precision Manufacturing

CAD/CAM, precision machining, and 3D-printing for custom hardware.

Conductive Nanomaterial Films

Silver and copper nanowire random networks for flexible, transparent devices.

Green Hydrogen Systems

Water electrolysis and hydrogen production toward green automotive technology.
